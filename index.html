What is real here

This is a working prototype, not a film. Everything below runs live in your browser.

Real

  • Your rear camera, live.
  • Your phone's motion sensors, used to detect a steady hold.
  • The dive transition, generated frame by frame.
  • Narration and timing, read from the script at runtime.
  • Stamps, saved on the device.

Simulated

  • The statue lock. Your tap stands in for recognising the animal's real shape. In the shipped version the phone tracks the sculpture itself.
  • The geofence. Off in demo mode so this works anywhere. On site it restricts the experience to the viewing spot.
